My story

Okay. Deep breath. Here's the truth.

For 18 years I looked like I had it ALL figured out. The finance career, the MBA, the life that photographs well. And inside? I was exhausted, anxious, and so far from myself I didn't recognize her anymore.

Back in 2004, my body tried to warn me. It gave out completely… a drug-induced seizure at my lowest, most depressed point, and I spent five days in the hospital. That should have been my wake-up call.

But you know what I did? I went right back to finance. For 15 MORE years. I got the sign, and I ignored it, because I didn't yet have the courage to change. Staying was familiar. Leaving was terrifying.

It took until 2019 for life to shake me awake for good. Grief came in waves that year… I lost three people who mattered so much to me. Two of them far, far too young. That was the year I finally walked away from finance entirely, and started the long walk back to myself.

I don't share this for sympathy. I share it because I know some of you are exactly where I was. Looking fine. Feeling empty. And I don't want it to take a hospital bed, or a loss, for you to remember there's SO much more.

The questions you're probably asking

Do I need any experience with breathwork or somatics?

None at all. Everything is guided, gently, step by step. Honestly? The women who've never done this before often go the deepest.

Is this therapy?

No. This is experiential work with the body, the breath & the subconscious. It's not diagnosis or treatment, but it can sit beautifully alongside the therapy you might already be doing.

What if I have a health condition?

Please just tell me when you register. Some breathwork isn't suitable for certain heart, seizure or psychiatric conditions, and pregnancy. I'll always adapt things so you feel safe & held.

How do I get to Ubud?

Fly into Bali (airport code DPS), then it's about a 1 to 1.5 hour drive up to Ubud. Once you've reserved, I'll send you everything… where to fly, where to stay, how to get to us.

What's the deposit & cancellation policy?

A 50% deposit reserves your space, with the balance due 60 days before we begin. All payments are non-refundable, but if life happens, your place can transfer to another woman, or become credit toward a future retreat within 12 months. I'd gently recommend travel insurance.

I'm nervous. Is that normal?

So normal. Nearly every woman feels it before she says yes. That flutter usually means something in you already knows it's time. Come anyway. I've got you.
